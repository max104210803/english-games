The New Kid 閱讀闖關

📖 先讀故事：The New Kid（藍色粗體是本課單字；闖關時忘了可以回來看）
🔊 聽故事朗讀（約 1 分 25 秒，AI 朗讀）：先聽一次，再跟著讀

Jay couldn't wait for the soccer club to start. He could not be patient because he loved chasing the ball across the field. On calm, peaceful Sunday mornings, he practiced in the park. He used his imagination to picture a big game, and then he kicked the ball as hard as he could.

On the first day, Jay said hello to everyone. He liked to interact and chat with his teammates. Then he saw a new boy named Ken. Ken entertained some kids by keeping the ball in the air with his knees. The kids clapped and laughed, but Jay was not amused.

Coach Wu blew his whistle. "Line up for races!" Jay felt so nervous that he had butterflies in his stomach. He had always been the fastest runner on the team. He ran hard, but he saw Ken getting ahead of him. Jay tried his best, but he couldn't match Ken. Ken won.

After practice, Jay's friend Ben asked, "Is the new kid really fast?" Jay held his tongue and said nothing.

That night, Dad asked, "Can you describe what's wrong?" "A new kid beat me in a race," Jay said. "It stinks to lose!" Dad smiled. "I know that's hard. But a fast player is good news for your team."

On Friday, the club played a practice game. Ken ran fast down the field and passed the ball to Jay. Jay thumped it hard toward the net. Goal! Ken gave Jay a high five. "Great kick!" he said. "Great pass!" Jay said. Then Jay smiled at Ken and said, "Our team can be great if we cooperate and work together."

看中文翻譯

Jay 等不及足球社開始了。他沒辦法耐心等，因為他太愛在球場上追著球跑。在安靜平和的星期天早上，他會到公園練球。他用想像力想像一場大比賽，然後用盡全力把球踢出去。

第一天，Jay 跟每個人打招呼。他喜歡和隊友互動、聊天。接著他看到一個叫 Ken 的新同學。Ken 用膝蓋把球一直頂在空中，逗得一些小朋友很開心。大家又拍手又笑，但 Jay 一點也不覺得好玩。

吳教練吹了哨子。「排好隊，準備賽跑！」Jay 緊張得心裡七上八下。他一直是隊上跑最快的人。他用力跑，卻看到 Ken 超到他前面。Jay 盡了全力，還是比不上 Ken。Ken 贏了。

練習結束後，Jay 的朋友 Ben 問：「那個新同學真的很快嗎？」Jay 忍住沒說話。

那天晚上，爸爸問：「你可以說說發生什麼事了嗎？」「有個新同學賽跑贏了我，」Jay 說。「輸掉真是糟透了！」爸爸笑了笑。「我知道這很難受。可是隊上有跑得快的球員，對你們球隊是好消息。」

星期五，足球社打了一場練習賽。Ken 快速帶球往前衝，把球傳給 Jay。Jay 用力一踢，球飛向球門。進球了！Ken 跟 Jay 擊掌。「踢得好！」他說。「傳得好！」Jay 說。接著 Jay 對 Ken 笑著說：「只要我們合作、一起努力，我們的球隊就會很厲害。」
