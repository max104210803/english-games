On Saturday, we went to Yehliu on the north coast. Taiwan is an island, so the sea is never far!

We saw the famous Queen's Head rock. Her neck is very thin now.

星期六，我們去了北海岸的野柳。台灣是一座島，所以海從來都不遠！
我們看到有名的女王頭。她的脖子現在好細。

A local guide told us about erosion. Wind and waves wash away tiny pieces of rock.

The rock looks solid, but it can still change over time.

Then we walked along the shore. The waves were very active that day.

一位當地的導覽員跟我們介紹侵蝕。風和海浪會把一點一點的小碎石沖走。
岩石看起來很堅固，但時間久了還是會改變。
接著我們沿著海岸走。那天的海浪很活躍。

I watched the waves explode on the rocks like fireworks! Mei screamed and ran away.

There were steep cliffs behind us. Dad said we must not climb them.

我看著海浪在岩石上炸開，像煙火一樣！Mei 尖叫著跑開。
我們後面是陡峭的懸崖。爸爸說我們絕對不可以爬上去。

At home, I looked at a little rock from the gift shop. I wrote down its properties: gray, hard, and rough.

The Earth is always changing, even when we can't see it.

回到家，我仔細看一顆在紀念品店買的小石頭，寫下它的特性：灰色的、硬硬的、粗粗的。
地球一直在改變，就算我們看不到也一樣。
