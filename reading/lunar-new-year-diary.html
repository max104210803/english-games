Every Lunar New Year, our family travels to Grandma's house in Tainan.

In the car, I said, "I wonder what Grandma will cook this year."

每年農曆新年，我們全家都會到台南的阿嬤家。
在車上，我說：「我好想知道阿嬤今年會煮什麼。」

On New Year's Eve, we were surrounded by food: hot pot, fish, and long-life vegetables.

Taiwan has many New Year customs. My favorite one is the red envelope!

Grandpa gave me a red envelope with both hands. Red is a common color at New Year.

除夕夜，我們被食物包圍：火鍋、魚，還有長年菜。
台灣有很多過年的習俗。我最喜歡的是紅包！
阿公用兩隻手把紅包交給我。紅色是過年常見的顏色。

The next day, we watched a lion dance at the temple.

Two dancers wore one big lion costume. The lion jumped and shook its head.

Mei hid behind me, but then she laughed and clapped.

隔天，我們在廟口看舞獅。
兩個舞者穿一套大大的獅子裝。獅子跳來跳去，還甩著頭。
Mei 躲到我後面，可是後來她笑著拍手。

Two weeks later, we went to the Lantern Festival. There were parades with music and lights.

Mei and I each carried a little paper lantern. It glowed in the dark!

The girl in our book and I have one thing in common. We both love New Year with family!

兩個星期後，我們去元宵燈會。有好幾支遊行隊伍，有音樂也有燈光。
Mei 和我各提著一個小小的紙燈籠。它在黑暗中發亮！
我和課本裡的女孩有一個共同點：我們都喜歡和家人一起過新年！
