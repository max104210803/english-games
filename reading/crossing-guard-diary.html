Our teacher gave us a new project: find a hero in our own community.

At first, I had no interest in this boring homework.

老師給了我們一個新作業：在自己的社區裡找一個英雄。
一開始，我對這個無聊的作業一點興趣都沒有。

Then I started to discover something surprising about our school.

Every rainy morning, old Mrs. Lin stands outside to help us cross the street.

Her job looked challenging, especially in strong wind and cold rain.

後來，我開始發現學校裡一件令人驚訝的事。
每個下雨天的早上，林奶奶都會站在外面幫我們過馬路。
她的工作看起來很有挑戰性，尤其是在強風和冷雨中。

I decided to study her daily schedule and ask her some questions.

"Many heroes do not wear capes," Mrs. Lin told me with a gentle smile.

我決定研究她每天的時間表，並且問她一些問題。
「很多英雄都不穿披風，」林奶奶對我露出溫柔的微笑說。

On Friday, I had to perform my project in front of the whole class.

My hands were shaking, but I wanted to succeed and make her proud.

After my talk, my classmates clapped loudly for Mrs. Lin.

We all agree that she is a true hero of our school.

星期五，我必須在全班面前表演（報告）我的作業。
我的手一直在抖，但我想要成功，讓她為我感到驕傲。
報告結束後，我的同學們為林奶奶大聲鼓掌。
我們都同意，她是我們學校真正的英雄。
