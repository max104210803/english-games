Monday. Today my social studies teacher gave us a big project. We have to tell a story about Taiwan four hundred years ago. Four hundred years! That is older than our school, our city, and even Grandpa.

After dinner, a package came from Tainan. It was from Grandpa. He was a history teacher before he retired. Inside, there was an old map and a short letter.

The letter said, "Dear Tim and Mei, you are now Formosa Time Detectives. Come to Tainan this winter. Find eight clues, and your project will be easy. Love, Grandpa."

星期一。今天社會老師給我們一個大作業：要說一個四百年前臺灣的故事。四百年！比我們學校、我們的城市，甚至比 Grandpa 還老。
晚餐後，臺南寄來一個包裹，是 Grandpa 寄的。他退休前是歷史老師。裡面有一張老地圖和一封短信。
信上寫：「親愛的 Tim 和 Mei，你們現在是福爾摩沙時光偵探了。這個寒假來臺南吧。找到八條線索，你們的報告就簡單了。愛你們的 Grandpa。」

Mei jumped up and down. "We are detectives!" she shouted. I opened the map on the table. It was not a map of Taiwan. It was a map of the whole world, with two long lines across the oceans.

Dad sat down next to us. He pointed to Europe. "About four hundred years ago, people in Europe loved things from Asia," he said. "They wanted silk, spices, and porcelain." Mom said spices could keep food fresh and make it taste better.

"But carrying these things over land was slow and very expensive," Dad said. "So the Europeans built better ships. They found new sea routes to Asia." People call this time the Age of Exploration. It was from the fifteenth to the seventeenth century.

Mei 跳上跳下，大喊：「我們是偵探！」我把地圖攤在桌上。那不是臺灣地圖，而是一整張世界地圖，上面有兩條橫越大海的長線。
爸爸坐到我們旁邊，指著歐洲說：「大約四百年前，歐洲人很喜歡亞洲的東西。他們想要絲綢、香料和瓷器。」媽媽說，香料可以讓食物保持新鮮，也讓味道更好。
爸爸說：「可是走陸路運這些東西又慢又貴，所以歐洲人造了更好的船，找到通往亞洲的新海路。」人們把這個時期叫做大航海時代，從十五世紀到十七世紀。

I followed the blue line with my finger. It went around the bottom of Africa and across the Indian Ocean. The red line went across the Pacific Ocean. Both lines ended near a small island. It was Taiwan!

"Why did they come here?" Mei asked. Dad smiled. "Taiwan is between Japan and Southeast Asia," he said. "So it was a perfect place for trade." When sailors saw our green mountains, they called Taiwan Formosa. It means "beautiful island."

At the bottom of the letter, Grandpa wrote one more thing. Long before the big ships, people in Taiwan already crossed the sea. Taiwanese jade was found in Southeast Asia! Today, the Taiwan Strait is one of the busiest shipping lanes in the world.

我用手指沿著藍線走：它繞過非洲南端，穿過印度洋。紅線則橫越太平洋。兩條線都停在一座小島附近——是臺灣！
Mei 問：「他們為什麼來這裡？」爸爸笑著說：「臺灣在日本和東南亞中間，所以是做生意的好地方。」水手看到我們綠色的山，就叫臺灣福爾摩沙，意思是「美麗之島」。
信的最下面，Grandpa 還多寫了一件事：在大船出現很久以前，臺灣的人就已經跨海交流了，東南亞還找到過臺灣的玉器！今天，臺灣海峽是世界上最繁忙的航道之一。

Before bed, I opened my new detective notebook. I wrote, "Clue number one. Europeans sailed to Asia for trade, and they called Taiwan Formosa."

Then I saw a small drawing on the back of the map. It was a red brick fort next to the sea. Under it, Grandpa wrote, "Your first stop. Can you find it?"

睡前，我打開新的偵探筆記本，寫下：「線索一：歐洲人為了貿易航行到亞洲，他們叫臺灣福爾摩沙。」
接著我看到地圖背面有一張小圖：海邊的一座紅磚城堡。Grandpa 在下面寫著：「你們的第一站。找得到嗎？」
