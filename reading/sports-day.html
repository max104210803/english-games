Scene 1: 出發挑戰與二手書攤

Sports Day had a new event this year: the Five-Dollar Eco-Challenge. Teams ran a coastal trail, made a video, and spent no more than five dollars.

At a used-book stall, Maya pointed to a nature guide. "Do you have one like this?" It was on sale for one dollar, so we bought it. We started plogging—jogging while collecting litter. We filled one bag instead of stopping for each and every wrapper.

今年運動會多了一個項目：「五美元生態挑戰」。各隊要跑完一段海岸步道、拍一支影片，而且總花費不能超過五美元。

在二手書攤，Maya 指著一本自然指南問：「你們有像這本一樣的書嗎？」那本書特價一美元，所以我們買了。我們開始 plogging（慢跑撿垃圾）：一邊慢跑、一邊撿拾垃圾。我們裝滿一袋垃圾，沒有為了每一個包裝紙都停下來。
Scene 2: 慢跑撿垃圾與花園安全提醒

Near the garden, Leo spotted a flower that looked like a trumpet and reached for it. Our guide stopped him from touching it: "That Brugmansia is poisonous." Leo pulled back. "I wanted a photo, not a hospital visit." After that, we thought twice before touching anything.

在花園附近，Leo 看到一朵看起來像喇叭的花，伸手想摘。導覽員阻止他碰它：「那是有毒的大花曼陀羅。」Leo 立刻縮回手。「我只是想拍照，可不想進醫院。」從那之後，碰任何東西前，我們都先想了兩次。
Scene 3: 步道地圖與跟錯零食推車

At the nature center, a ranger gave us a map: "Follow the blue arrows." We tried to do so, but Leo followed a snack cart. Our team split up; two classmates nearly ended up stranded across a stream. We reunited and banned Leo from choosing the route.

在自然中心，巡護員給了我們一張地圖：「沿著藍色箭頭走。」我們試著照做，但 Leo 卻跟著一台零食推車走了。全隊分頭找他時，兩位同學差點受困在小溪的另一邊。我們最後會合，並禁止 Leo 再替大家選路線。
Scene 4: 海景咖啡館與最佳音效獎

The tiny café above the harbor had a great view, but lunch cost an arm and a leg. We had four dollars left, so we ate our apples outside and enjoyed the same view for free.

It took an hour to get through editing our video: Leo added dramatic music to every clip—even his sneeze. We came last in the race but won "Best Sound Effects."

港口上方的小咖啡館景色很棒，但午餐貴得要命。我們只剩四美元，就在外面吃蘋果，免費欣賞同一片海景。

我們花了一小時才完成影片剪輯：Leo 替每段影片都加上戲劇配樂——連他打噴嚏的片段也不例外。我們跑步比賽得了最後一名，卻拿下「最佳音效獎」。

🎯 本篇關鍵考點語塊（11 組）

語塊 / 片語 中文釋義 原創故事中的用法
one like this 像這個一樣的（同款） “Do you have one like this?”
on sale 特價中 The nature guide was on sale for one dollar.
plogging 慢跑撿垃圾（jogging + picking up litter） Our first task was plogging.
each and every 每一個（加強語氣） We didn't stop for each and every wrapper.
look like 看起來像…… A flower looked like a trumpet.
stop sb. from V-ing 阻止某人做某事 Our guide stopped him from touching it.
think twice before V-ing 做……之前先三思 We thought twice before reaching for any plant.
do so 這麼做（代替前述動作） We tried to do so, but Leo followed a snack cart instead.
end up + 形容詞／分詞 最後落得……的結果 They nearly ended up stranded on the wrong side of a stream.
cost an arm and a leg 貴得要命（索價極高） The lunch special cost an arm and a leg.
get through 完成、做完 It took us an hour to get through editing our video.
