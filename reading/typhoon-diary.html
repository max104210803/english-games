Today the weather was super weird. The sky was dark, and the wind was getting harsh.

On TV, there was a typhoon warning. Mom pointed at the window. "It is dangerous to go outside tonight," she said.

今天的天氣超怪。天空很暗，風也越來越猛。
電視上發布了颱風警報。媽媽指著窗戶說：「今晚出去很危險。」

But there was a BIG problem. Grandma's osmanthus tree was full of flowers. A typhoon can cause a lot of damage to them.

So we put a big mat under the tree and shook the branches with long sticks. The flowers fell like yellow rain!

可是有個大問題。奶奶的桂花樹開滿了花，颱風會把它們打壞（造成很大的損害）。
所以我們在樹下鋪了一張大蓆子，用長竿子搖樹枝。桂花像黃色的雨一樣落下來！

My little sister Mei got flowers all over her hair. She looked like a walking birthday cake.

我妹妹 Mei 滿頭都是桂花，看起來像一個會走路的生日蛋糕。

That night, the wind was SO loud. I thought it would destroy our whole house.

It didn't. But it broke Dad's umbrella. Dad said a typhoon is a big event in Taiwan.

We can't prevent a typhoon. But we CAN save the flowers. Tomorrow Mom will make osmanthus tea. I can't WAIT.

那天晚上，風聲好大。我以為它會把我們整間房子毀掉。
結果沒有。不過爸爸的雨傘被吹壞了。爸爸說，颱風在台灣是一件大事。
我們沒辦法阻止颱風，但我們「可以」救下桂花。明天媽媽要泡桂花茶，我等不及了！
