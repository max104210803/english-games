Sunday. It rained a little this morning, but we did not stay home. Detectives do not stop because of rain!

Grandpa took us to Chihkan Tower. It has red walls, curved roofs, and old stone turtles in the yard. "This building used to be a Dutch fort," Grandpa said. "Its old name was Fort Provintia."

"Long ago, the sea came almost to this place," he said. "Fort Zeelandia was on the other side of the water." I looked around. Now there are only roads, shops, and scooters.

星期日。早上下了一點雨，但我們沒有待在家。偵探不會因為下雨就停下來！
Grandpa 帶我們去赤崁樓。它有紅色的牆、彎彎的屋頂，院子裡還有古老的石龜。Grandpa 說：「這棟建築以前是荷蘭人的城堡，舊名叫普羅民遮城。」
「很久以前，海水幾乎到這裡，」他說，「熱蘭遮城就在水的另一邊。」我看看四周，現在只剩馬路、商店和機車。

We sat on a bench, and Grandpa told us the story. "The Dutch gave Han Chinese farmers land to grow sugarcane and rice. But life was not easy. Dutch soldiers often treated the farmers badly."

"Then, one year, there was no rain for a long time. It was a terrible drought. The rice and sugarcane grew very little. The farmers had less money, so they could not pay their taxes."

Mei frowned. "That is not fair," she said. Grandpa nodded. "Many farmers felt the same way."

我們坐在長椅上，Grandpa 說起故事：「荷蘭人給漢人農民土地種甘蔗和稻米。但日子不好過，荷蘭士兵常常欺負農民。」
「後來有一年，很久都沒下雨，是很嚴重的乾旱。稻米和甘蔗幾乎長不出來。農民的錢變少了，所以繳不起稅。」
Mei 皺起眉頭：「這樣不公平。」Grandpa 點點頭：「很多農民也這麼覺得。」

"In 1652, a farmer named Guo Huaiyi decided to fight back," Grandpa said. "He led many farmers against the Dutch. They were very brave."

"Did they win?" I asked. Grandpa shook his head slowly. "No. The Dutch had guns, and the farmers did not. The farmers lost the fight."

"After that, the Dutch were worried," Grandpa said. "So the next year, in 1653, they built Fort Provintia to protect themselves." That is why this tower is here today.

「一六五二年，一位叫郭懷一的農民決定反抗，」Grandpa 說，「他帶著很多農民對抗荷蘭人。他們很勇敢。」
我問：「他們贏了嗎？」Grandpa 慢慢搖頭：「沒有。荷蘭人有槍，農民沒有。農民輸了。」
「之後荷蘭人很擔心，」Grandpa 說，「所以隔年，一六五三年，他們蓋了普羅民遮城來保護自己。」這就是這座樓今天還在這裡的原因。

I asked about a strange name on a sign. It said "Red Hair Tower." Grandpa smiled. "Many Europeans had red or golden hair. So Han people called the Dutch red hair."

Mei touched her own black hair and giggled. "Then I am a black hair detective," she said.

I felt a little sad all afternoon. The farmers worked so hard, but they still lost. I want to remember Guo Huaiyi in my project.

That night, I wrote in my notebook. "Clue number three. After Guo Huaiyi's fight in 1652, the Dutch built Fort Provintia."

Grandpa looked at my notebook and smiled. "Only nine years later, many ships came from the west," he said. "They were not Dutch ships. Can you guess whose ships they were?"

我問到告示牌上一個奇怪的名字：「紅毛樓」。Grandpa 笑著說：「很多歐洲人頭髮是紅色或金色的，所以漢人叫荷蘭人紅毛。」
Mei 摸摸自己的黑頭髮，咯咯笑：「那我是黑毛偵探。」
整個下午我都有點難過。農民那麼努力，卻還是輸了。我想在報告裡記住郭懷一。
那天晚上我在筆記本寫下：「線索三：一六五二年郭懷一反抗之後，荷蘭人蓋了普羅民遮城。」
Grandpa 看著我的筆記本笑了：「才過九年，很多船從西邊開過來。不是荷蘭人的船。你猜得到是誰的船嗎？」
