It was winter vacation. We drove up to Hehuan Mountain to see snow!

The temperature★ was below freezing. My breath looked like smoke. Mei thought she was a dragon.

寒假到了。我們開車上合歡山看雪！
氣溫在冰點以下。我呼出的氣看起來像煙，Mei 以為自己是一條龍。

Mei and I wanted to build an igloo★, like a real ice house. We made snow blocks with a box.

But the blocks kept falling apart. Then I remembered snice★ from English class. It is a mix of snow and ice.

We put wet snow between the blocks. It held everything together!

我和 Mei 想蓋一間冰屋，像真的冰做的房子。我們用一個箱子壓出雪磚。
可是雪磚一直散掉。這時我想起英文課學到的 snice：雪和冰的混合物。
我們在雪磚之間塞進濕濕的雪，結果全部黏在一起了！

Our igloo was very small. Only Mei could fit inside. She called it her ice hotel.

我們的冰屋很小，只有 Mei 鑽得進去。她說這是她的冰旅館。

In the afternoon, the sun came out. Our igloo started to melt.

Mei was sad. Dad said ice cannot stay long when it gets above freezing. Next winter, we will build a bigger one.

下午太陽出來了，我們的冰屋開始融化。
Mei 好難過。爸爸說，氣溫一升到冰點以上，冰就撐不久。明年冬天，我們要蓋一間更大的。
