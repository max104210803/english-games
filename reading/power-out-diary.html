This weekend, we visited Grandma. She lives in one of the small villages in the quiet countryside.

At night, the lights went out! The whole village was dark. Mei started to cry. She does not like the dark.

Grandma had no flashlight. So I ran across the street to borrow one from Uncle Lee.

這個週末，我們去看奶奶。她住在安靜鄉下的一個小村莊。
晚上，燈突然熄了！整個村子一片黑。Mei 開始哭，她不喜歡黑漆漆的。
奶奶家沒有手電筒，所以我跑到對面，向李伯伯借了一支。

But the flashlight was too weak. Then I had an idea. I put it under a big bottle of water.

The whole room lit up! Mei said it looked like a moon in a bottle.

可是手電筒太暗了。這時我想到一個點子：我把它放在一大瓶水的下面。
整個房間都亮了起來！Mei 說它看起來像裝在瓶子裡的月亮。

Now she insists on keeping the "bottle moon" next to her bed. She even gave it a name: Moony.

現在她堅持要把「瓶中月亮」放在床邊，還幫它取了名字：Moony。

Later, Grandma told me something. She sometimes feels lonely here at night. I think she needs a solution too.

So I made a second bottle moon for her. Grandma laughed and gave me a big hug.

Note to self: Ask Dad about solar lights for Grandma's house!

後來奶奶跟我說，她晚上一個人在這裡有時會覺得孤單。我想，她也需要一個解決辦法。
所以我又做了第二個瓶中月亮給她。奶奶笑了，給我一個大大的擁抱。
給自己的提醒：問爸爸奶奶家能不能裝太陽能燈！
