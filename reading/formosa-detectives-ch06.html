Monday. This morning, we walked through a big red gate. Above it, there were four Chinese words. Grandpa read them slowly. "The first school of all Taiwan."

星期一。今天早上我們走過一座紅色大門，門上有四個中文字。Grandpa 慢慢念出來：「全臺首學。」

Inside, it was very quiet. Old trees made cool shadows on the ground. Birds sang softly. Mei forgot about running and started to whisper.

"This is the Confucius Temple," Grandpa said. "It was the first Confucius Temple in Taiwan. It was also one of the first schools here." We walked to a large hall with a golden roof. People come to this hall to honor Confucius.

"Who built it?" I asked. "Koxinga's son, Zheng Jing," Grandpa said. "But the idea came from his teacher, Chen Yonghua." Some people call Chen Yonghua a very wise helper, like Zhuge Liang.

"Chen Yonghua gave Zheng Jing three ideas," Grandpa said. He counted on his fingers. "Build a Confucius temple. Open schools to teach reading and old books. And give tests to choose good officials."

"Tests?" Mei made a face. Grandpa laughed. "Yes. Smart people could pass the tests and help run the land." Because of these ideas, Han Chinese culture began to grow in Taiwan.

After lunch, we went to a small temple called Yonghua Temple. "People built it to remember Chen Yonghua," Grandpa said. I bowed to say thank you.

裡面非常安靜。老樹在地上投下涼涼的影子，鳥兒輕輕地唱歌。Mei 忘了要跑，開始小聲說話。
Grandpa 說：「這是孔廟。它是臺灣第一座孔廟，也是這裡最早的學校之一。」我們走到一座有金色屋頂的大殿，人們來這裡紀念孔子。
我問：「是誰蓋的？」Grandpa 說：「鄭成功的兒子鄭經。不過點子來自他的老師陳永華。」有人把陳永華比作很有智慧的幫手，就像諸葛亮。
Grandpa 扳著手指數：「陳永華給鄭經三個點子：蓋孔廟；開學校，教讀書和古書；還有用考試選出好官員。」
Mei 做了個鬼臉：「考試？」Grandpa 笑了：「對。聰明的人通過考試，就能幫忙治理地方。」因為這些點子，漢人文化開始在臺灣成長。
午餐後，我們去一間小廟，叫永華宮。Grandpa 說：「人們蓋這間廟來紀念陳永華。」我鞠躬說謝謝。

In the afternoon, we drove north to the train station. On the way, I saw a road sign. It said "Linfengying." "Grandpa, what does ying mean?" I asked.

"Ying means army camp," he said. "Remember, Koxinga's army needed food. So soldiers spread out to many places around Tainan. When there was no war, they were farmers. When a war came, they became soldiers again."

"They also brought farmers from Fujian," he said. "But they could not take land from Han farmers or Indigenous people." Today, many place names with ying or zhen came from these soldiers. Zuozhen is in Tainan, and Zuoying is in Kaohsiung.

下午我們開車往北去火車站。路上我看到一個路牌，寫著「林鳳營」。我問：「Grandpa，營是什麼意思？」
「營就是軍隊的營地，」他說，「還記得嗎？鄭成功的軍隊需要糧食，所以士兵分散到臺南附近很多地方。沒有打仗時，他們是農夫；戰爭來了，他們又變回士兵。」
「他們也從福建帶來農民，」他說，「但不能拿走漢人農民或原住民族的土地。」今天很多有「營」或「鎮」的地名，都來自這些士兵，例如臺南的左鎮、高雄的左營。

At the station, Grandpa hugged us. "I will visit you in Taipei next weekend," he said. "Your last forts are in the north."

On the train, I wrote, "Clue number six. Chen Yonghua helped Zheng Jing build the Confucius Temple, schools, and tests. Ying and zhen place names came from farming soldiers."

在車站，Grandpa 抱抱我們：「下週末我去臺北看你們。最後的城堡在北部。」
在火車上我寫下：「線索六：陳永華幫鄭經蓋孔廟、辦學校、辦考試。『營』『鎮』地名來自屯墾的士兵。」
