Saturday. Grandpa came to Taipei this morning. He brought his magnifying glass and the old map. "Detectives, look at the red line," he said.

The red line crossed the Pacific Ocean. It stopped at Manila, in today's Philippines. Then it went north to Taiwan. "This was the route of the Spanish," Grandpa said.

"The Dutch were in the south," Grandpa said. "But in 1626, the Spanish landed in the north." I remembered my first clue. The Dutch were in the south, and the Spanish were in the north!

星期六。今天早上 Grandpa 來到臺北，帶著放大鏡和那張老地圖。他說：「偵探們，看這條紅線。」
紅線橫越太平洋，停在今天菲律賓的馬尼拉，然後往北到臺灣。Grandpa 說：「這是西班牙人的路線。」
「荷蘭人在南部，」Grandpa 說，「但在一六二六年，西班牙人從北部登陸。」我想起第一條線索：荷蘭在南、西班牙在北！

First, we took the MRT to Tamsui. We walked up a hill to a red building. It was Hongmao Castle. "The Spanish built the first fort here," Grandpa said. "They called it Fort Santo Domingo. Later, it was rebuilt many times."

"Hongmao means red hair," Mei said proudly. "Han people called the Dutch red hair!" Grandpa clapped. "You remember Chapter Three!"

我們先搭捷運到淡水，走上一座小山丘，到一棟紅色的建築，是紅毛城。Grandpa 說：「西班牙人在這裡蓋了第一座城，叫聖多明哥城。後來被重建了很多次。」
Mei 得意地說：「紅毛就是紅頭髮。漢人叫荷蘭人紅毛！」Grandpa 拍手：「妳記得第三章！」

In the afternoon, we drove to Keelung. We visited Heping Island, next to the sea. Long ago, it was called Sheliao Island. "The Spanish landed here first," Grandpa said. "They built Fort San Salvador at the harbor."

"Why did they come?" I asked. "For trade, just like the Dutch," Grandpa said. "They bought silk and porcelain from Chinese traders."

Near the harbor, we saw a sign about an old church. "The Spanish built a church here called All Saints Church," Grandpa said. "They taught people about Catholicism."

"Wait," I said. "The Dutch taught Christianity, right?" Grandpa nodded. "Yes. Both of them spread their religions. But the Dutch taught Christianity, and the Spanish taught Catholicism."

下午我們開車到基隆，參觀海邊的和平島。很久以前它叫社寮島。Grandpa 說：「西班牙人最先在這裡登陸，在港口蓋了聖薩爾瓦多城。」
我問：「他們為什麼來？」Grandpa 說：「為了做生意，跟荷蘭人一樣。他們向中國商人買絲綢和瓷器。」
港口附近有一塊關於老教堂的告示牌。Grandpa 說：「西班牙人在這裡蓋了一座教堂，叫諸聖教堂，向人們傳天主教。」
我說：「等等，荷蘭人傳的是基督教，對吧？」Grandpa 點頭：「對。他們兩個都傳宗教，但荷蘭人傳基督教，西班牙人傳天主教。」

On the way home, Dad drove along the northeast coast. We passed a cape called Sandiaojiao. "Say Sandiaojiao fast," Grandpa said. "Now say Santiago." Mei tried. "They sound alike!" she laughed. The name came from the Spanish word.

"So what happened to the Spanish?" Mei asked. "In 1642, the Dutch wanted all the trade for themselves," Grandpa said. "So they sailed north and drove the Spanish away."

That night, I wrote, "Clue number seven. The Spanish came to northern Taiwan in 1626. They built forts and a church. The Dutch drove them away in 1642."

I counted my clues. Seven! "One more clue," Grandpa said. "But this time, you must find it yourself."

回家路上，爸爸沿著東北海岸開車，經過一個叫三貂角的海角。Grandpa 說：「快快念三貂角，再念 Santiago。」Mei 試了一下，笑著說：「聽起來好像！」這個名字就是從西班牙文來的。
Mei 問：「那西班牙人後來怎麼了？」Grandpa 說：「一六四二年，荷蘭人想要獨占所有生意，所以往北航行，把西班牙人趕走了。」
那天晚上我寫下：「線索七：西班牙人一六二六年來到北臺灣，蓋了城堡和教堂。一六四二年被荷蘭人趕走。」
我數數我的線索：七條！Grandpa 說：「還差一條。不過這一次，你要自己找。」
