Sunday. I spread my seven clues on my desk. Mei sat next to me with a box of colored pencils. "What is the last clue?" she asked. I did not know.

I read all my clues again. They were like puzzle pieces. Then I had an idea. "The last clue is not a place," I said. "It is the order of the story!"

Mei and I drew a long line on a big piece of paper. We wrote the years one by one. Mei colored the Dutch part orange and the Spanish part red.

First, 1624. The Dutch landed in Tainan and built Fort Zeelandia. Then, 1626. The Spanish landed in Keelung, in the north.

Next, 1642. The Dutch drove the Spanish away. Then, 1652. Guo Huaiyi led the farmers, but they lost. After that, the Dutch built Fort Provintia.

Then, 1661. Koxinga's ships came, and the Dutch gave up in 1662. Koxinga set up the first Han Chinese government. In the end, in 1683, the Qing Empire took over Taiwan.

Mei looked at the line. "It looks like a train," she said. "Each year is a train car." I liked that idea, so I drew wheels under the years.

星期日。我把七條線索攤在書桌上。Mei 拿著一盒色鉛筆坐在旁邊，問：「最後一條線索是什麼？」我不知道。
我把所有線索再讀一遍，它們像拼圖的碎片。然後我想到了：「最後一條線索不是一個地方，是故事的順序！」
Mei 和我在一大張紙上畫了一條長線，一個一個寫上年份。Mei 把荷蘭的部分塗成橘色，西班牙的部分塗成紅色。
首先，一六二四年。荷蘭人登陸臺南，蓋了熱蘭遮城。接著，一六二六年。西班牙人在北部的基隆登陸。
再來，一六四二年。荷蘭人把西班牙人趕走。接著，一六五二年。郭懷一帶領農民反抗，但失敗了。之後荷蘭人蓋了普羅民遮城。
接著，一六六一年。鄭成功的船隊來了，荷蘭人在一六六二年投降。鄭成功建立第一個漢人政府。最後，一六八三年，清帝國統治臺灣。
Mei 看著那條線說：「看起來像一列火車，每個年份都是一節車廂。」我喜歡這個點子，就在年份下面畫了輪子。

Monday. Today was project day. My hands were cold, but I stood in front of the class. I showed our time line and the old map.

"About four hundred years ago, Europeans sailed to Asia for trade," I said. "They called Taiwan Formosa because it was beautiful."

Then I told the class what each group left behind. "The Dutch brought mangoes, wax apples, peas, and cattle. They also taught Christianity. The Spanish taught Catholicism. They left Hongmao Castle and the name Sandiaojiao. Koxinga's family brought farming soldiers, schools, and the Confucius Temple."

星期一。今天是報告日。我的手很冰，但我還是站到全班面前，展示我們的時間線和老地圖。
我說：「大約四百年前，歐洲人為了做生意航行到亞洲。他們叫臺灣福爾摩沙，因為它很美。」
接著我告訴全班每個族群留下了什麼：「荷蘭人帶來芒果、蓮霧、豌豆和黃牛，也傳基督教。西班牙人傳天主教，留下紅毛城和三貂角這個名字。鄭成功一家帶來屯墾的士兵、學校和孔廟。」

My classmates asked many questions. One boy asked, "Which came first, the Dutch or the Spanish?" I smiled. "The Dutch, in 1624. But only two years earlier!"

My teacher said, "Wow, Tim, you can be our tour guide!" Everyone clapped. Mei was not in my class, but I knew she would be proud.

同學問了很多問題。一個男生問：「荷蘭人和西班牙人誰先來？」我笑著說：「荷蘭人，一六二四年。不過只早了兩年！」
老師說：「哇，Tim，你可以當我們的導覽員了！」大家都拍手。Mei 不在我們班，但我知道她會以我為榮。

After school, I called Grandpa on video. "Case closed, Grandpa!" I said. He laughed and held up his magnifying glass.

That night, I wrote my last clue. "Clue number eight. History is a long time line. Every place has a story, if you look closely."

放學後，我跟 Grandpa 視訊：「案件結束了，Grandpa！」他笑著舉起放大鏡。
那天晚上我寫下最後一條線索：「線索八：歷史是一條長長的時間線。只要仔細看，每個地方都有故事。」
