1. On Monday, the weather was strange. The sky was dark far away, and the wind was getting harsh. Then Ms. Hill, our teacher, heard a tornado warning on the radio.

星期一的天氣很奇怪。遠方的天空很暗，風也越來越猛烈。接著，我們的老師 Hill 老師從收音機聽到了龍捲風警報。

2. "It is dangerous to stay near the windows," she said. "Let's go to the basement, just like in our tornado drill!" We walked quickly and quietly. But wait, where was Buddy, our class dog?

「待在窗戶旁邊很危險，」她說。「我們去地下室，就像龍捲風演習時那樣！」我們又快又安靜地走過去。可是等等，我們班的狗 Buddy 在哪裡？

3. Ms. Hill found Buddy under her desk. He was shaking. She picked him up and carried him to the basement. We sat together and listened. The wind sounded like a very loud train.

Hill 老師在她的桌子底下找到 Buddy。牠在發抖。她把牠抱起來，帶到地下室。我們坐在一起聽著外面，風聲聽起來像一列非常吵的火車。

4. After the storm passed, we went outside. The tornado did not destroy our school, but it caused some damage to the big tree. "A tornado is a big event," said Ms. Hill. "Following safety rules can help us prevent harm." Buddy wagged his tail. We were all safe!

暴風雨過去後，我們走到外面。龍捲風沒有毀掉我們的學校，但讓那棵大樹受到一些損害。「龍捲風是一件大事，」Hill 老師說。「遵守安全規則可以幫我們預防傷害。」Buddy 搖著尾巴。我們全都平安！
