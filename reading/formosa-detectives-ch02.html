Saturday. We took the high-speed train to Tainan this morning. Mei held the old map the whole time. She was afraid to lose it.

Grandpa was waiting for us at the station. He wore a funny hat and carried a magnifying glass. "Good morning, detectives!" he said. "Are you ready for your first stop?"

星期六。今天早上我們搭高鐵去臺南。Mei 一路抱著那張老地圖，怕弄丟。
Grandpa 在車站等我們，他戴著一頂好笑的帽子，拿著放大鏡。「早安，偵探們！」他說，「準備好去第一站了嗎？」

We took a taxi to Anping. Soon, I saw a tall tower and old red brick walls. It looked just like the drawing on the back of the map! "This is Anping Fort," Grandpa said. "Long ago, it had another name. It was called Fort Zeelandia."

我們搭計程車到安平。不久，我看到一座高塔和舊紅磚牆，跟地圖背面的畫一模一樣！Grandpa 說：「這是安平古堡。很久以前它有另一個名字，叫熱蘭遮城。」

We sat under a big banyan tree. Grandpa opened the map and pointed to the blue line. "This is the route of the Dutch," he said. "The Dutch came from the Netherlands, a small country in Europe."

"First, they stayed in Batavia," Grandpa said. "Today, Batavia is in Indonesia. Then they sailed north to Penghu. But the Ming Empire drove them away. Finally, they landed here in Anping in 1624."

"Why did they want this place?" I asked. Grandpa said, "They wanted a base for trading with China and Japan. Tainan was in the middle of the busy sea." So the Dutch built Fort Zeelandia as their trading base.

Mei asked, "Did the Dutch live here alone?" Grandpa shook his head. "No. They ruled the Indigenous people nearby. They also brought many Han Chinese farmers from China."

The farmers grew sugarcane and rice. The Dutch sold the sugar to other countries and made a lot of money. "So sugar was like gold back then," Mei said. Grandpa laughed. "You are a smart detective."

我們坐在一棵大榕樹下。Grandpa 打開地圖，指著藍線說：「這是荷蘭人的路線。荷蘭人來自荷蘭，歐洲的一個小國家。」
「他們先待在巴達維亞，」Grandpa 說，「巴達維亞在今天的印尼。然後他們往北航行到澎湖，可是被明帝國趕走。最後，他們在一六二四年從安平登陸。」
我問：「他們為什麼想要這裡？」Grandpa 說：「他們想要一個和中國、日本做生意的據點。臺南就在忙碌大海的中間。」所以荷蘭人蓋了熱蘭遮城當作貿易據點。
Mei 問：「荷蘭人自己住在這裡嗎？」Grandpa 搖搖頭：「不是。他們統治附近的原住民族，也從中國帶來很多漢人農民。」
農民種甘蔗和稻米。荷蘭人把糖賣到其他國家，賺了很多錢。Mei 說：「所以那時候糖就像黃金一樣。」Grandpa 笑了：「妳是個聰明的偵探。」

We climbed the stairs to the top of the tower. The wind was strong, and I could see the sea. I tried to imagine big Dutch ships with white sails.

I touched the old red brick wall. It was rough and warm in the sun. These bricks are almost four hundred years old. I took a picture for my project.

That night, I wrote in my detective notebook. "Clue number two. The Dutch landed in Tainan in 1624 and built Fort Zeelandia."

Before bed, Grandpa showed us an old drawing of Tainan. There was another fort across the water. "Tomorrow, you will hear a sad story there," he said quietly.

我們爬樓梯到塔頂。風很大，我看得到海。我試著想像掛著白帆的荷蘭大船。
我摸了摸舊紅磚牆，粗粗的，在太陽下暖暖的。這些磚頭快四百歲了。我拍了一張照片放進報告。
那天晚上，我在偵探筆記本寫下：「線索二：荷蘭人在一六二四年登陸臺南，蓋了熱蘭遮城。」
睡前，Grandpa 給我們看一張臺南的老圖，水的對面還有另一座城堡。他小聲說：「明天，你們會在那裡聽到一個難過的故事。」
