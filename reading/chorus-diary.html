Today was the big chorus contest at our school. I was SO nervous.

Our class practiced for two weeks. Ms. Chen played the piano for us. The piano is my favorite instrument. Its sounds are soft and warm.

今天是學校的合唱大賽，我超級緊張。
我們班練了兩個星期。陳老師幫我們彈鋼琴。鋼琴是我最喜歡的樂器，它的聲音柔和又溫暖。

But I have a secret. I do not understand rhythm very well. Sometimes I sing too fast.

So Ms. Chen taught me a trick. I tap my foot to keep the beat.

可是我有個祕密：我不太懂節奏，有時候會唱太快。
所以陳老師教我一個小技巧：用腳輕輕踏拍子，保持節拍。

We also had hand movements. During practice, I almost hit Kevin in the face. Oops.

我們還有手勢動作。練習的時候，我差點打到 Kevin 的臉。糟糕。

On stage, the music started. I tapped my foot and sang my heart out.

When we finished, everyone cheered! It felt like a real concert.

We won second place. Kevin forgave me. I think.

上台後，音樂開始了。我一邊踏腳，一邊盡情地唱。
唱完的時候，大家都在歡呼！感覺就像一場真正的音樂會。
我們得了第二名。Kevin 原諒我了。我想是吧。
