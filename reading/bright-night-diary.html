Today we read about Alaska in English class. Mr. Lin asked me to find its location on the globe.

It is far from Taiwan. Northern Alaska is in the cold Arctic region.

今天英文課讀了阿拉斯加。林老師叫我在地球儀上找出它的位置。
它離台灣很遠。阿拉斯加北部在寒冷的北極地區。

After dinner, Mei and I had a video call with Uncle Wei. He works in Alaska this summer.

It was ten at night there, but the sky was still bright! It felt a little eerie.

Uncle Wei laughed. He said the Sun does not set for many days in summer.

晚餐後，我和 Mei 跟偉叔叔視訊。他今年夏天在阿拉斯加工作。
那邊已經晚上十點了，天空卻還是亮的！感覺有點毛毛的。
偉叔叔笑了。他說夏天的時候，太陽很多天都不會下山。

He wore three layers of clothes, even in summer. Behind him was a thick growth of ferns.

His lively dog jumped up and barked at us. Mei waved at the dog.

他穿了三層衣服，就算是夏天也一樣。他後面有一大叢長得很密的蕨類。
他那隻活潑的狗跳起來對著我們叫。Mei 對著狗揮手。

Uncle Wei said summer in Alaska is temperate, not too hot or too cold.

Summer in Taiwan is very hot! Our seasons are so different.

In winter, his town will be dark almost all day. I still want to visit him someday!

偉叔叔說，阿拉斯加的夏天很溫和，不會太熱也不會太冷。
台灣的夏天超熱的！我們的季節差好多。
到了冬天，他住的小鎮幾乎整天都是黑的。我還是想找一天去看他！
