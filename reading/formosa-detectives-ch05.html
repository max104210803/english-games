Sunday night. The night market was bright, noisy, and full of good smells. Mei held my hand so she would not get lost.

"Detectives, find three treasures from the Dutch," Grandpa said. "You have twenty minutes. Go!"

星期日晚上。夜市又亮又吵，到處都是香味。Mei 牽著我的手，免得走丟。
Grandpa 說：「偵探們，找出三個荷蘭人留下的寶物。你們有二十分鐘，出發！」

We stopped at a fruit stand first. There were big yellow mangoes and red wax apples. "Did the Dutch bring these?" I asked. Grandpa nodded. "Yes! Mangoes and wax apples came with the Dutch."

Mom bought a bag of sweet peas at another stand. "In Taiwanese, people sometimes call peas Dutch beans," Grandpa said. "That is our second treasure!"

我們先停在水果攤。有大大的黃色芒果和紅色蓮霧。我問：「這是荷蘭人帶來的嗎？」Grandpa 點頭：「對！芒果和蓮霧是跟著荷蘭人來的。」
媽媽在另一攤買了一袋甜豌豆。Grandpa 說：「臺灣人有時候把豌豆叫做荷蘭豆。這是第二個寶物！」

Mei pointed at a picture on a beef noodle shop wall. It was a cow pulling a plow. "The Dutch also brought cattle," Grandpa said. "Cattle helped farmers plow the fields. So farmers could grow more food." That was treasure number three.

We sat down to eat. Then Grandpa told us something sad. "Back then, many sika deer lived in Taiwan. The Dutch wanted their skins."

"The Dutch gave the Indigenous people cloth and other daily things," he said. "In return, they got deer skins. Then they sold the skins to Japan for a lot of money."

"People hunted too many deer," Grandpa said. "Han farmers also set traps and turned the deer's land into farms. Soon there were very few deer left." Mei put down her chopsticks. "Poor deer," she said quietly.

Mei 指著牛肉麵店牆上的一張圖，是一頭牛在拉犁。Grandpa 說：「荷蘭人也帶來了黃牛。黃牛幫農夫耕田，農夫就能種更多糧食。」這是第三個寶物。
我們坐下來吃東西。接著 Grandpa 說了一件難過的事：「那時候臺灣有很多梅花鹿，荷蘭人想要牠們的皮。」
「荷蘭人給原住民族布和其他日常用品，」他說，「換來鹿皮，再把鹿皮賣到日本賺很多錢。」
「人們捕了太多鹿，」Grandpa 說，「漢人農民也設陷阱，還把鹿住的地方開墾成田。很快地，鹿就剩下很少了。」Mei 放下筷子，小聲說：「可憐的鹿。」

Grandpa showed us a picture of an old book on his phone. It had two kinds of words. One side was Dutch. The other side was an Indigenous language written with Roman letters.

"The Dutch wanted to teach Christianity," Grandpa said. "So they wrote down Indigenous languages with Roman letters." Even later, people still used these letters to write land papers.

I was surprised. "So the Dutch left fruit, cattle, and even letters," I said. Grandpa smiled. "And some sad lessons, too."

Before we went home, I bought one more wax apple. It was sweet and crunchy.

That night, I wrote, "Clue number five. The Dutch brought mangoes, wax apples, peas, and cattle. They also sold so many deer skins that the deer almost disappeared."

Grandpa looked at my notebook and said, "Tomorrow, let's visit a very quiet place. You must not run there." Mei groaned. Running is her favorite thing.

Grandpa 用手機給我們看一本舊書的照片。書上有兩種文字：一邊是荷蘭文，另一邊是用羅馬字母寫的原住民族語言。
「荷蘭人想要傳基督教，」Grandpa 說，「所以他們用羅馬字母寫下原住民族的語言。」就連後來，人們還用這種字母寫土地契約。
我很驚訝：「所以荷蘭人留下了水果、黃牛，連字母都有。」Grandpa 笑著說：「還有一些令人難過的教訓。」
回家前，我又買了一顆蓮霧，又甜又脆。
那天晚上我寫下：「線索五：荷蘭人帶來芒果、蓮霧、豌豆和黃牛。他們也賣了太多鹿皮，梅花鹿差點消失。」
Grandpa 看著我的筆記本說：「明天我們去一個很安靜的地方，在那裡不可以跑。」Mei 哀嚎了一聲，跑步是她最愛的事。
