Sunday afternoon. "I know! They were Koxinga's ships!" I shouted at lunch. Grandpa clapped his hands. "Good guess, detective!"

星期日下午。午餐時我大喊：「我知道了！是鄭成功的船！」Grandpa 拍手：「猜得好，偵探！」

After lunch, we visited a quiet shrine with tall trees. A big statue of Koxinga stood inside. He looked strong and serious. Mei whispered, "He looks like a general."

Grandpa told us the story. "At that time, China had big changes. The Ming Empire fell, and the Qing Empire took over. Koxinga wanted to fight the Qing and bring back the Ming."

"But he lost many battles in China," Grandpa said. "So he needed a new home base. He also wanted to keep his sea trade. And his army needed food." Taiwan was close to Fujian, so he looked across the sea.

午餐後，我們參觀一座有高大樹木的安靜祠堂。裡面有一尊很大的鄭成功像，看起來強壯又嚴肅。Mei 小聲說：「他看起來像一位將軍。」
Grandpa 說起故事：「那時候中國發生很大的變化。明帝國滅亡，清帝國接手。鄭成功想要對抗清帝國，讓明朝回來。」
「可是他在中國打輸了很多仗，」Grandpa 說，「所以他需要一個新的根據地。他也想維持海上貿易，而且他的軍隊需要糧食。」臺灣離福建很近，所以他望向海的對岸。

"In 1661, his ships sailed to Tainan," Grandpa said. "His army attacked Fort Provintia first. Then they surrounded Fort Zeelandia." I remembered both forts. We saw them yesterday!

"The Dutch fought for many months," Grandpa said. "But in 1662, they gave up and left Taiwan." Koxinga even let them take their food and things with them.

Then Koxinga set up Chengtian Prefecture in Chihkan. It was the first Han Chinese government in Taiwan. "So the red tower became his office," I said. Grandpa gave me a thumbs-up.

"Why do people call him the Lord of the Royal Surname?" Mei asked. "A Ming emperor liked him very much," Grandpa said. "The emperor gave him the royal family name." People also called him the Prince of Yanping. That is why this place is called Yanping Prince Shrine.

I also learned that Koxinga's father was Zheng Zhilong. He was a rich sea trader with many ships. Koxinga's son, Zheng Jing, traded with Japan, China, and Southeast Asia. He sold deer skins and sugar to Japan.

「一六六一年，他的船隊開到臺南，」Grandpa 說，「軍隊先攻打普羅民遮城，接著包圍熱蘭遮城。」這兩座城我都記得，我們昨天才看過！
「荷蘭人打了好幾個月，」Grandpa 說，「但在一六六二年，他們投降離開臺灣。」鄭成功甚至讓他們帶走糧食和財物。
接著鄭成功在赤崁設立承天府，這是臺灣第一個漢人政府。我說：「所以那座紅色的樓變成他的辦公室了。」Grandpa 對我比讚。
Mei 問：「為什麼大家叫他國姓爺？」Grandpa 說：「一位明朝皇帝很欣賞他，把皇家的姓賜給他。」人們也叫他延平郡王，所以這裡叫延平郡王祠。
我還知道鄭成功的爸爸是鄭芝龍，一位擁有很多船的富有海商。鄭成功的兒子鄭經和日本、中國、東南亞做生意，把鹿皮和糖賣到日本。

On the way out, Mei asked about Turtle Island in Yilan. "Did Koxinga shoot a giant turtle there?" she asked. Grandpa laughed. "That is only a story. Koxinga never went to Yilan."

That night, I wrote, "Clue number four. Koxinga drove the Dutch away in 1662 and set up the first Han Chinese government."

Mom looked at the clock. "Time for dinner," she said. Grandpa winked at us. "Tonight, we will find treasure at the night market. Some of it came from the Dutch."

離開時，Mei 問起宜蘭的龜山島：「鄭成功真的在那裡射了一隻大烏龜嗎？」Grandpa 笑了：「那只是傳說。鄭成功從來沒去過宜蘭。」
那天晚上我寫下：「線索四：鄭成功在一六六二年趕走荷蘭人，建立第一個漢人政府。」
媽媽看看時鐘：「該吃晚餐了。」Grandpa 對我們眨眨眼：「今晚我們要去夜市找寶藏，有些寶藏是荷蘭人帶來的。」
